A new look. Components are rounder (buttons and fields 12px, cards 24px), flatter (no resting shadow on inputs and cards) and outlined at 1.5px. In light mode, neutrals move from cool blue-grey to warm stone, the page background (`bg-canvas`) is a warm beige with white cards on it, and danger, success and warning use new, more vivid fills with darker text that meets WCAG AA in every state. Dark mode keeps its colours and only changes shape; cards keep their outline there. Primary blue, typography and every prop are unchanged.

To migrate, put `bg-canvas` on your page root if you haven't already: in light mode the default Card (`variant="outlined" color="neutral"`) is white with no visible border and needs the canvas behind it to stand out. On another white surface, add `className="border-neutral-outlined-border"`; the new `--color-card-outlined-neutral-border` token carries that border (transparent in light, the outline in dark). Token names are otherwise unchanged; token values are not, so anything you built from `--color-neutral-*`, `--color-surface*`, `--radius-*` or `--shadow-xs` changes with it. If you build with `@hintoric/ui/tailwind.css`, its theme sets `--default-border-width: 1.5px`, so a bare `border`, `border-t` or `divide-*` in your own markup becomes 1.5px too; write `border-1` where you want a 1px rule. Emails follow the same palette and radii, except that the email Card keeps its outline in light (every email Card sits on a white card); `emailPalettes` and `emailOutlineWidth` are new exports. Update `@hintoric/icons` along with it: its light illustrations move to the same warm greys.
