The light illustrations now use the warm greys of `@hintoric/ui`'s new light look instead of the cool blue-greys and lavender tints of the old one, so they no longer show as a cold patch on the beige `bg-canvas`. Each grey keeps its place on the ramp, so an illustration keeps its contrast. The white sticker outline and every accent colour (blue, green, yellow, coral and the periwinkle text lines) are unchanged, and so are the dark variants. If you stay on the previous `@hintoric/ui` look, stay on the previous `@hintoric/icons` too: on the old light page the new illustrations look slightly warm.

The `cdn.light` files, `FormReadingLensAnimatedIcon` and the `company-data-found` image in `@hintoric/email`'s `CompanyDataFoundEmail` load from the CDN, which now serves the warm versions to every release. The previous files are still there under `https://cdn.hintoric.com/assets/icons/<slug>/joy/`.
